Generated by `nulltest/read_classification.py` from the offline suite's saved results. Do not edit by hand: the class
and the method of a stage are set in the script, from [MEASUREMENTS.md](../MEASUREMENTS.md#measured-laws); the numbers
are the results'.

16 stages over 82 offline cases, from the run of 2026-10-21: 12 exact, 4 fittable. The reading is the stage's worst
case on the scale of [MEASUREMENTS.md](../MEASUREMENTS.md#setup). 6 stages read below a NULL: EQ; Ring modulation;
Precomputed cut and resonance; Pogo; Envelope; Filter types.

No target and no round budget were set per stage when the stages were fitted, so the sheet has neither column. A stage
with no offline case has no row: the time-stretch modes, which cannot null (the reference's engine is proprietary) and
are compared by behaviour, and the features tested only as pairs inside the DAW, whose figures stand beside their laws
in MEASUREMENTS.md.
