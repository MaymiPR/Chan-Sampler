Chan Sampler for macOS (universal: Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 10.13 or later)
==================================================================================

The easiest way is the installer (ChanSampler-<version>-macOS.pkg on the
Releases page). It is not notarized by Apple (that needs a paid developer
account), so macOS stops it the first time: see "Opening it anyway" below.
To copy the files by hand instead:

1. Drag the plugins into your plugin folders (in Finder: Go > Go to Folder...):
   - AU   (Logic, GarageBand, Ableton Live, FL Studio...):
         "Chan Sampler.component"  ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
   - VST3 (Ableton Live, FL Studio, Reaper, Cubase...):
         "Chan Sampler.vst3"       ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
   - CLAP (Bitwig, Reaper...):
         "Chan Sampler.clap"       ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/
   - Standalone: drag "Chan Sampler.app" to Applications.

2. Allow them to run. macOS blocks downloaded copies until the download
   "quarantine" flag is removed. Open Terminal and paste:

     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"Chan Sampler.component"
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"Chan Sampler.vst3"
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/"Chan Sampler.clap"
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/"Chan Sampler.app"

3. Restart your DAW (Logic rescans AUs at launch; in Ableton use
   Settings > Plug-ins > Rescan).

Opening it anyway (the installer or the app)
- macOS 15 (Sequoia) and later: open it once (macOS says it can't check it),
  then go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
  "Open Anyway" next to the Chan Sampler message, and confirm.
- Earlier macOS: right-click (or Control-click) it, choose Open, then Open.

To check that your download is the one published, compare its SHA-256 with
SHA256SUMS.txt on the Releases page: in Terminal, run  shasum -a 256 <file>
