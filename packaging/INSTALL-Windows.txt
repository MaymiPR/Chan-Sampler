Chan Sampler for Windows (64-bit)
=================================

The easiest way is the installer (ChanSampler-<version>-Windows-Setup.exe on
the Releases page). To copy the files by hand instead:

Plugins
1. Copy them to the standard plugin folders (Windows asks for administrator
   permission):
   - VST3: the whole "Chan Sampler.vst3" folder  ->  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
   - CLAP: "Chan Sampler.clap"                   ->  C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP\
2. Rescan plugins in your DAW:
   - FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins
   - Reaper: Preferences > Plug-ins > VST (or CLAP) > Re-scan
   - Bitwig: picks up new plugins by itself

Standalone
- "Chan Sampler.exe" runs without a DAW.

"Windows protected your PC"
Chan Sampler is not code-signed (that needs a paid certificate), so Windows
SmartScreen may warn the first time you run the installer or the app. Click
"More info", then "Run anyway". To check that your download is the one
published, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt on the Releases page: in
PowerShell, run  Get-FileHash <file>
