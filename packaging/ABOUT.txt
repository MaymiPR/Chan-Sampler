Chan Sampler
============

Chan Sampler is a free, open-source project (AGPL-3.0): https://github.com/MaymiPR/Chan-Sampler

It is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Image-Line.
FL Studio is a trademark of Image-Line Software; it is named only to describe what Chan Sampler is
modelled on. None of Image-Line's code, artwork, samples, preset files, fonts or other files is
included: Chan Sampler was built from measurements of how FL Studio's sampler sounds and behaves,
and its editor is drawn by its own code.
